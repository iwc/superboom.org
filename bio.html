Bio

Irwin Coleman is an experienced software developer living in Atlanta, Georgia.

Irwin graduated from the Georgia Institute of Technology with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science in 1996, and later earned his Master of Science in Computer Science in 1999. His studies focused on User Interface software, ubiquitous computing, computer graphics, computer vision and networking.

Irwin has developed user interface software for over a decade, for companies such as Nortel Networks, ESPN, AT&T, Samsung and Yahoo!.

Irwin has been an early startup employee at multiple startups, and seeks out innovative new projects

Irwin is happily married to his wife Maribeth and has a daughter, two cats and a dog. When not working, Irwin likes running, books, music and movies.
